About the Westworld Quiz

Think you know everything about Westworld? Put your knowledge to the test with this 60-question trivia quiz.

The questions cover Hosts, characters, parks, organizations, AI systems, locations, identities and memorable events from across the television series.

Each question has four possible answers. A correct answer earns points, while an incorrect answer earns no points. The maximum possible score is 100.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Westworld quiz work?

You will face 60 questions about Westworld. Each question has four possible answers, but only one answer is correct.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100 points. Your final score is based on the number of questions you answer correctly.

What does my score mean?

Your score represents how well you remember the characters, Hosts, parks, AI systems, locations and major events of Westworld.

What topics are covered?

The quiz covers characters, Hosts, theme parks, organizations, artificial intelligence, locations, identities, relationships and major events from across the series.

Does the quiz cover all four seasons?

Yes. The questions cover a broad range of characters, events, locations and storylines from all four seasons of the television series.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to replay it and try for a higher score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the Challenge Your Friends button after completing the quiz and see if they can beat your score.
