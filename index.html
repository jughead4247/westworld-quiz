About the Westworld Quiz

Think you know everything about Westworld? Put your knowledge to the test with this 60-question trivia quiz covering Hosts, characters, parks, artificial intelligence, technology, identities, organizations and major events from the series.

The questions are designed for viewers who want to test how closely they remember the complicated world of Westworld. You may be asked about Dolores, Bernard, Maeve, William, Ford, the Man in Black and other important characters, as well as the different parks, Hosts and systems that shape the story.

Some questions focus on major storylines, while others test smaller details that can be easy to forget. Multiple timelines, changing identities, revelations and connections between characters make the series particularly challenging to remember.

How the Westworld Quiz Works

The quiz contains 60 multiple-choice questions. Each question has four possible choices, so read the wording carefully before selecting your answer. The questions cover different characters, locations, technologies, Hosts and events from across the series.

Your result is calculated from your correct answers and converted into a percentage, with a maximum score of 100. A higher score means you remembered more of the show's characters, storylines, mysteries and smaller details.

The quiz is intended as entertainment and fan trivia, rather than an official assessment. If you make mistakes, you can replay the quiz and try to improve your result. Fans who have watched the series more than once may have an advantage when questions involve details that were deliberately revealed later in the story.

Quiz Navigation

Select START QUIZ to begin. The quiz displays your current question number and a progress bar while you work through all 60 questions.

Choose the answer you believe is correct and use Next to continue. The Back button allows you to return to an earlier question when available. Pay particular attention to names, identities, relationships, locations and events because Westworld often connects details that initially appear unrelated.

After completing the quiz, your result screen displays your final score and Westworld Knowledge level. You can select TRY AGAIN to replay the quiz, use SHARE MY RESULT to share your performance, or choose CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS to see whether another fan can score higher.

Westworld Trivia and FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The Westworld quiz contains 60 questions, with four possible choices for each question.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100. Your result is calculated from your correct answers and converted into a percentage.

What does the quiz cover?

Questions cover Hosts, human characters, parks, artificial intelligence, technology, organizations, identities, relationships, locations and major events from Westworld.

Does the quiz cover all four seasons?

Yes. The quiz covers material from all four seasons, including characters, storylines, locations and developments introduced throughout the series.

Why can Westworld be difficult to remember?

The series uses multiple timelines, changing identities, Hosts with different roles and revelations that are gradually explained. Details that seem unimportant at first can become significant later, making the show especially challenging for trivia fans.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and attempt to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. Use the CHALLENGE YOUR FRIENDS option after completing the quiz and see whether your friends can beat your Westworld knowledge score.

Spoiler Warning

This quiz contains spoilers for Westworld. Questions may reveal important characters, identities, relationships, Host developments, locations, parks, organizations and major plot events from the series.

If you have not finished watching the relevant seasons, consider completing them before taking the quiz to avoid having important story details and revelations spoiled.
