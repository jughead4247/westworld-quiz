About the Westworld Quiz

Think you know everything about Westworld? Put your knowledge to the test with this 60-question trivia quiz covering Hosts, characters, parks, artificial intelligence, technology, identities and major events from across the series.

The quiz is designed for viewers who want to test how well they remember the complicated world of Westworld. Some questions focus on major characters and storylines, while others test details about the technology, different parks, organizations and mysteries that become important throughout the series.

Each question has four possible answers and only one is correct. Your final score is converted to a percentage, with 100 representing a perfect score. Because the series contains multiple timelines, changing identities and increasingly complicated technology, remembering the smaller details can be just as difficult as remembering the major events.

Artificial Intelligence and Conscious Machines

One of the central ideas explored by Westworld is whether an artificial intelligence could eventually become genuinely conscious. Modern artificial intelligence systems can recognize patterns, generate language, analyze images and make decisions, but these abilities do not necessarily mean that a machine has subjective experience.

A conscious machine would raise much deeper questions. Could it understand itself? Could it experience fear or pain? Could it develop personal goals that were never programmed by its creators? And if an artificial being could experience the world for itself, would humans have a responsibility to protect its rights?

These questions become particularly important as robots become more capable of interacting naturally with people. A machine that looks, speaks and behaves like a human could make it increasingly difficult to determine where convincing imitation ends and genuine consciousness begins.

Robotics, Automation and Human Dependence

Advanced robotics could transform many parts of society. Robots are already used in manufacturing, warehouses, hospitals, agriculture and dangerous environments. Future systems could become capable of performing increasingly complex physical tasks with limited human supervision.

Automation can make work safer and more efficient, but it can also create new forms of dependence. If important systems rely heavily on autonomous machines, failures could have consequences far beyond a single malfunctioning robot.

A highly automated society would therefore need safeguards, human oversight and reliable emergency systems. The more responsibility given to machines, the more important it becomes to understand what those machines are allowed to do and what happens when their decisions conflict with human interests.

Human Behavior, Free Will and Predictive Systems

Another major question raised by Westworld is whether human decisions are truly free or whether behavior can be predicted from enough information about a person.

Modern technology already collects enormous amounts of behavioral data. Search history, purchases, location information, social media activity and online interactions can reveal patterns about what people like, fear or are likely to do next.

Predictive systems can be useful when applied to areas such as traffic management, fraud detection, medical research or disaster planning. However, using predictions to control individual choices creates difficult ethical questions. A prediction about what someone is likely to do is not necessarily proof of what they will actually do.

This creates an important distinction between predicting human behavior and controlling it. The more powerful predictive technology becomes, the more important transparency, privacy and human choice become.

Digital Worlds, Data and the Future of Society

Westworld also explores what could happen when artificial environments become almost indistinguishable from reality. Virtual worlds, advanced simulations and immersive technologies could eventually allow people to spend large amounts of time inside digitally created environments.

Digital worlds could have useful applications in education, entertainment, scientific research, training and communication. A realistic simulation could allow people to practice dangerous situations without facing the real-world consequences.

However, highly realistic digital environments would also create new questions about identity, privacy and ownership. Who controls the data generated inside a virtual world? Who owns an artificial personality? And if an intelligent digital being exists inside a simulated environment, does deleting that environment amount to destroying a form of life?

Westworld Trivia and FAQ

How many questions are in the quiz?

The Westworld quiz contains 60 questions with four possible choices for each question. The questions cover the television series across all four seasons.

What is the maximum score?

The maximum score is 100. Your result is calculated from the number of correct answers and converted into a percentage.

Is the quiz only about the main characters?

No. Questions can involve Hosts, humans, parks, technology, artificial intelligence, organizations, locations, relationships and important events. Some questions require remembering relatively small details from the series.

Does the quiz include all four seasons?

Yes. The quiz covers material from all four seasons, including the changing worlds, characters and technologies introduced as the story progresses.

Why is Westworld difficult to remember?

The series uses multiple timelines, changing identities, Hosts that can have different roles and storylines that are deliberately revealed over time. This makes seemingly small details important and can make a second viewing very different from the first.

Can I replay the quiz?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after finishing the quiz to restart it and attempt to improve your score.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Your Friends button to share the challenge and see whether someone else can score higher than you.
